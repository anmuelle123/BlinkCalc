# BlinkCalc Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 1, 2026

BlinkCalc is designed to be a privacy-friendly calculator for macOS.

## Data Collection

BlinkCalc does not collect, sell, track, or share personal data. The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, user accounts, or tracking technologies.

## Local Data

BlinkCalc stores calculator history, preferences, selected exchange-rate provider, hotkey settings, and cached exchange rates locally on your Mac. This data stays on your device and is not sent to BlinkCalc or the developer.

## Live Exchange Rates

If you use live currency conversion, BlinkCalc connects to public exchange-rate services to fetch current rates. These requests may disclose technical request information, such as your IP address and the requested currency pair, to the selected exchange-rate provider. BlinkCalc does not receive, store, or track this information.

## Network Access

BlinkCalc uses network access only to fetch live exchange rates. All other calculations, including standard calculator functions, scientific calculations, percentages, units, and local history, run on your Mac.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local BlinkCalc data remains on your Mac until you clear it in the app or uninstall the app. You can clear calculation history from within BlinkCalc.
